Welcome to the EIC Tutorial on kinematic reconstruction!

This tutorial walks you through accessing the reconstructed inclusive kinematics in the ePIC
simulation output, benchmarking the performance of the different reconstruction methods, and
implementing and optimising the reconstruction yourself.

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: prereq

## Prerequisites

- Knowledge of CERN ROOT and/or Python/RDataFrames.
- This tutorial follows earlier tutorials in the EIC series, in particular
  [Setting Up Your Environment](https://eic.github.io/tutorial-setting-up-environment/).

Please take a look at the [Setup](learners/setup.md) page for necessary prerequisites for this
lesson.
