## Glossary

EIC
:   Electron-Ion Collider

DIS
:   Deep Inelastic Scattering

HFS
:   Hadronic Final State, the collection of all final-state particles except the scattered electron.

Inclusive kinematics
:   The event-level variables `x`, `y`, `Q2`, and `W` that describe a DIS event, reconstructed in
    the ePIC output in the `InclusiveKinematicsXX` branches (where `XX` is the reconstruction
    method: Truth, Electron, JB, DA, Sigma, or ESigma).
