---
title: Setup
---

If you have not done so already, please follow the instructions in the
[Setting Up Your Environment tutorial](https://eic.github.io/tutorial-setting-up-environment/)
well before the start of the tutorial to ensure your system is ready.

You will need a working `eic-shell` and enough disk space to download a few simulation files
(a few hundred MB each) from the Jefferson Lab xrootd server.
