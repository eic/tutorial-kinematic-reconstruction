---
title: 'Learner Profiles'
---

This lesson targets students, postdocs, and researchers in the ePIC collaboration who need to work
with the reconstructed inclusive kinematics (`x`, `y`, `Q2`, `W`) in the simulation output. Learners
are expected to have a working `eic-shell` and some familiarity with CERN ROOT and/or
Python/RDataFrames, but no prior experience with the ePIC data model (`edm4eic`/`podio`) or the
different kinematic reconstruction methods is assumed.
